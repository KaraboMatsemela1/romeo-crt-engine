## Purpose

This matrix prevents the project from silently blending earlier and later Romeo material into one invented rule set. Each tension is classified as one of:

- `REFINEMENT` — later material appears to narrow or operationalize earlier teaching
- `CONTEXT_DEPENDENT` — both statements may be true in different parent/setup states
- `POTENTIAL_CONTRADICTION` — direct-source verification is required before implementation
- `SCOPE_DECISION` — project chooses a narrower v0.1 boundary without claiming broader doctrine is false
- `UNRESOLVED`

No row may be resolved by profitability optimization.

## Reconciliation principles adopted

1. **Later operational clarification may narrow earlier conceptual teaching, but never silently.**
2. **A scope decision is not a claim that excluded Romeo setups are invalid.**
3. **When two examples can be explained by nested parent CRTs, encode parent IDs/timeframes before declaring contradiction.**
4. **No contradiction may be resolved by selecting the version that backtests better.**
5. **Any rule depending on a visual distinction must receive direct visual/source verification before implementation.**
6. **`UNRESOLVED` means fail closed.**
