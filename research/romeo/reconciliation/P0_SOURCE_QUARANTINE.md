# P0 Source Quarantine

**Purpose:** prevent unverified, private, leaked, re-uploaded or derivative materials from silently entering the Romeo public-strategy specification.

## Quarantine policy

A source may be used for executable rule closure only when its provenance is sufficiently established as:

1. Romeo official public YouTube/channel material;
2. Romeo official Telegram/X/Instagram material;
3. a reliable index of an already verified Romeo-primary public source.

A source is quarantined when:
- the title/uploader claims Romeo but official provenance is missing;
- it appears to be a private mentorship/session recording;
- it may be a third-party re-upload;
- the indexed transcript is available but the original source identity is uncertain.

Quarantined material may generate research questions, but **may not close strategy rules**.

Rich detail is not a substitute for trustworthy provenance.

```text
UNVERIFIED SOURCE
      ↓
QUARANTINE
      ↓
QUESTION GENERATION ONLY

not

UNVERIFIED SOURCE
      ↓
DETAILED TRANSCRIPT
      ↓
STRATEGY RULE
```
